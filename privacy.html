Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026

Welcome to Arogia. We understand that health data is deeply personal. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to protecting your privacy and explains how your data is handled when you use our AI-powered medical report analysis tool.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Arogia, we collect two types of information:

  • Uploaded Reports: Images (JPG, PNG) or PDFs of your medical lab results that you voluntarily upload for analysis.
  • Usage Data: Basic analytics such as IP address, browser type, and time spent on the site to help us improve the platform (via standard cookies).

2. How We Handle Medical Data (Strict No-Save Policy)

We treat your medical reports with the highest level of security. Arogia is designed as a pass-through analysis tool.

  • Your uploaded files are temporarily processed in memory to extract text (OCR) and generate the AI summary.
  • Automatic Deletion: All files and extracted data are permanently and automatically deleted from our servers within a maximum of 24 hours.
  • We do not use your personal medical records to train public AI models.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide our services, we may use trusted third-party APIs (such as OCR and LLM providers). These providers are strictly vetted and are bound by confidentiality agreements that prohibit them from storing or using your data for their own purposes.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Arogia uses essential cookies to ensure the website functions correctly. We do not use tracking cookies to serve you targeted healthcare advertisements across the internet.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Because we do not permanently store your data, there are no long-term databases of your health records to manage. However, if you have signed up for an account (if applicable), you have the right to request the complete deletion of your profile and email address at any time.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our privacy team at privacy@arogia.com.
