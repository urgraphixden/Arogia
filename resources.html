Glossary
Medical Terms, Explained Simply

Search instantly through trusted medical databases like Mayo Clinic, WHO, and WebMD.

Hemoglobin

A protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen around your body. Low levels can mean anemia.

Vitamin D

A vitamin your body makes from sunlight. It keeps bones strong and supports your immune system.

HbA1c

A blood test that shows your average blood sugar level over the past 2-3 months.

Creatinine

A waste product filtered by your kidneys. High levels can indicate your kidneys aren't filtering well.

Cholesterol (LDL/HDL)

Fat-like substances in your blood. LDL is "bad" cholesterol, HDL is "good" cholesterol that protects your heart.

TSH (Thyroid)

A hormone that tells your thyroid gland how much thyroid hormone to make. Used to check thyroid health.

Platelets

Tiny blood cells that help your blood clot and stop bleeding when you're injured.

Vitamin B12

A vitamin needed for healthy nerves and red blood cells. Low levels can cause tiredness and weakness.

Blood Pressure

The force of blood pushing against your artery walls. Written as two numbers, like 120/80.
